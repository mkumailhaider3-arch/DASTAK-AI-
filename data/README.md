# DASTAK AI — Datasets & Licensing Documentation

> **Important Notice:** Never put private real CNICs, passports, or personal documents in this repository. All local demo files in `data/sample_synthetic/` are 100% synthetic fictional examples generated by `scripts/prepare_data.py`.

## Research Datasets Considered (External)

The following public research datasets are relevant to document understanding, OCR, and classification. They are **general document research datasets**, **not Pakistani CNIC verification datasets**:

| Dataset | Domain / Task | Access & Licence Notes | Intended Use & Limitations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RVL-CDIP** | General document image classification (16 classes, ~400k grayscale scans) | Publicly distributed for non-commercial academic research (IIT Complex Document Information Processing collection). | Useful for benchmarking general document layout classification. Not representative of South Asian identity documents; noisy low-res scans. |
| **FUNSD** | Form understanding & key-value pair linking (199 scanned forms) | Research/academic use (check official FUNSD licence terms before redistribution). | Useful for testing key-value extraction and form layout parsing. Small sample size. |
| **SROIE** | Scanned receipt OCR & key information extraction (ICDAR 2019) | Research competition dataset (check ICDAR/task terms). | Useful for receipt OCR and date/total extraction evaluation. Limited to English/Malaysian store receipts. |
| **CORD** | Consolidated Receipt Dataset for post-OCR parsing | CC BY 4.0 (verify upstream repository terms). | Useful for structured receipt parsing. Not applicable to identity cards or academic transcripts. |

## Synthetic Local Dataset (`data/sample_synthetic/`)

Because external datasets do not cover Pakistani-style synthetic pre-checking packages and contain no privacy-safe local identity samples, `scripts/prepare_data.py` generates synthetic fictional images (`.png`) and metadata (`dataset_manifest.csv`) across the 5 prototype categories:
- `identity-card-style sample`
- `passport-style sample`
- `educational certificate`
- `application form`
- `receipt/other`

All names, dates of birth, and ID numbers are fictional placeholders (e.g., `Ayesha Khan`, `ID-00000-0000000-0`).
